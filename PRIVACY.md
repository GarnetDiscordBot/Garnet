# Garnet Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

> **Draft:** This Privacy Policy is currently a draft and should be reviewed and updated to accurately reflect Garnet's actual data collection and storage before public release.

This Privacy Policy explains how Garnet may process information when you use the bot.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, Garnet may process information such as:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Discord role IDs
- Configuration and settings created by server administrators
- Information required to operate individual Garnet features

Garnet should only store information that is reasonably necessary for its features.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide Garnet's features.
- Remember server configuration.
- Operate and maintain the bot.
- Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

Garnet does not need to know a user's real-world identity to provide its normal Discord functionality.

## 3. Data Sharing

Garnet does not intend to sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure required to operate Garnet where necessary. The final policy should be updated to identify any such providers before public release.

## 4. Data Retention and Deletion

The exact retention periods depend on the information and feature involved.

The final public policy will document how users and server administrators can request deletion of applicable stored information.

## 5. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by Garnet. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Security issues can be reported according to the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## 6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Garnet's features or data practices change.
